Juara original IP

Events we own and grow ourselves.

Beyond delivering for clients, Juara builds and operates its own sports properties, from concept and sanctioning to race day.

Roller skating championship · Prestasi & Gembira

Rockets Rollers is Juara’s own roller skating championship, run under PB PORSEROSI rules with PORSEROSI Jawa Timur. It combines professional-standard competition with a festival for athletes, coaches, clubs, schools, supporters, and sponsors, built to grow interest in the sport.

  • Series #1, Sidoarjo, 31 May – 1 June 2025
  • Series #2, Track Sepatu Roda Kanjuruhan, Malang, 3–5 October 2025
  • Competition: Beginner 50 m & 100 m, Standard 300 m, 500 m & 1,000 m, Speed 3,000 m & 5,000 m, Speed Slalom, and Skate Cross
  • Beyond the race: participant parade, team presentation, exhibition performance, clinics, brand activation, and tenant bazaar

Aquabike Indonesian Championship

Kejurnas Aquabike · IMI-sanctioned national series

Indonesia’s national jet ski championship, developed after the Aquabike World Championship at Lake Toba in 2023 to give Indonesian riders a path to world-level competition while promoting priority tourism destinations. Co-promoted with Ocean Drive Sports.

  • 2024 series: Toba (24–25 Feb), Samosir (27–28 Jul), North Sulawesi (31 Aug – 1 Sep), South Sulawesi (21–22 Sep)
  • 2025 Round 1, Bandengan Beach, Jepara, 11–13 July 2025, Piala Menpora
  • Around 30 riders per series, racing Endurance and Parallel Slalom
  • Opening ceremony, flag parade, flyboard show, gala dinner, and press conference, covered by Kemenpora, Metro TV, detikJateng, and Tribun
